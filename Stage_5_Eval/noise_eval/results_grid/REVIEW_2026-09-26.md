## RANKED FINDINGS (most severe first)

1. MAJOR / CONFIRMED - Live runner silently drops the last turn when the server never closes it.
   Evidence: gemini_live_transcribe.py::stream lines 78-87 (audioStreamEnd sent, then loop exits after
   --idle 30 s once sender is done, regardless of an open turn). results_grid/live/raw: 7 files end with
   START=END+1 and a trailing interimInputTranscription as the last message (rt0.4_snrnone_d3/d4,
   rt0.8_snrnone_d0/d4, rtnone_snr0_d0, rtnone_snr10_d0, rtnone_snr5_d0). Effect: 1.4-1.6 pp WER on five of
   them (e.g. d0 table Live 45.4 -> 43.8 at 10 dB, 50.2 -> 48.8 at 0 dB), always against Live.
   Fix: pad 1-2 s of zeros before audioStreamEnd; after the sender finishes, keep receiving until the open
   turn gets ACTIVITY_END/generationComplete (or a longer cap); if a turn is still open at exit, keep the
   last interim and flag the file. Re-score the 7 files.

2. MAJOR / CONFIRMED - RESULTS.md noisy-VAD table has a stale CI column. 6 of 14 rows' "[lo, hi]" differ
   from results_2026-09-24/result_vad_cells.csv (none/20: [28.5,43.2] vs csv [29.6,42.4]; none/10 [32.1,49.3]
   vs [33.1,48.6]; none/5 [34.9,59.5] vs [36.2,58.5]; none/0 [39.7,73.3] vs [40.4,73.0]; 0.4/none
   [68.1,86.4] vs [69.2,86.3]; 0.8/0 [93.2,99.0] vs [93.4,99.0]). Point estimates, delta and VAD-cost columns
   match; the tracked result_vad_table.md and a fresh score.py regen (byte-identical to all four CSVs) agree
   with the CSV. Fix: paste the CI column from result_vad_table.md.

3. MAJOR / CONFIRMED (statistic), SUSPECTED (cause) - Reference noise is undisclosed and material.
   87 of 1046 GT words (8.3%) are produced by none of six systems on clean; GT line 1 "tom melihat mary
   memecahkan kaca jendela" is 6/6 never produced, line 87 5/12, line 44 3/7. Cause is most likely reference
   text without matching audio (pseudo-label or mis-cut). Effect: ~8 pp floor on every absolute WER; the
   "35.5% clean" headline and "Finding 1" absolute numbers overstate error; paired deltas are largely immune.
   Fix: listen to those GT lines against the audio; report a multi-system agreement ceiling; disclose in
   Known limitations.

4. MINOR / CONFIRMED - METHODS §4.4 "temperature 0 is the default for every system" is false for the two
   Transcribe systems: gemini_transcribe.py::call sets generationConfig only when a prompt is given, so
   gemini-3.5-transcribe requests carry no temperature (raw confirms no generationConfig); Live has none.
   Fix: say so, or send temperature 0 if the model accepts it.

5. MINOR / CONFIRMED - RESULTS grid-Live claim "The 21 files carried over from the subset run reproduce its
   numbers exactly" is a tautology: the 21 .txt and all 23 raw .jsonl (incl. two fail logs) are
   byte-identical because they were copied. Fix: reword as "reused unchanged" (as the Transcribe section does).

6. MINOR / CONFIRMED - Whisper prose overstates: "that one segment ('eh' x 384) is most of the WER" - it is
   384 of 1011 errors (38%; WER without it 59.9%, not 96.7%). "One looping segment in each of two files" -
   rtnone_snr0_d0 fallback has two (209 and 101 words). Also "Full grid ~20 h" assumes greedy minimum;
   fallback mean 30 min x 71 = ~36 h.

7. MINOR / CONFIRMED - wer.py's alignment is not min-edit-distance. __global_alignment maximises
   2M - S - 0.75(I+D), i.e. minimises 3S + 3.5D rather than S + 2D. Constructed case: 142.9% vs Levenshtein
   100%. On real transcripts: +1..+11 errors (<=1.1 pp), largest on insertion-heavy outputs (Whisper loop
   file +11). Consistent across systems but slightly penalises loopers more. Disclose or use Levenshtein.

8. MINOR / CONFIRMED - Undisclosed Live retry: rtnone_snr10_d2 needed three attempts (socket dropped at
   159 s and again later); .fail1/.fail2 logs exist in both result dirs. Transient by the stated policy, but
   §Retries does not list it and "no stream failed in this run" is true only of the 50 new grid files.

9. MINOR / CONFIRMED - normalise() keeps word-internal apostrophes; the reference contains none. Baseline
   outputs carry 12-35 such tokens per dir ("ka'ab", "ta'ala", "qur'an", "don't"), each a guaranteed error
   for baselines only (<=0.5 pp/file). Live finals also contain CamelCase joins ("kiriDalamOpa", 0-10 per
   file) that normalise cannot split. Fix: strip apostrophes (GT style); optionally split [a-z][A-Z].

10. MINOR / CONFIRMED - d0 table "Gemini 3 Flash 0.8s = 42.5" is double-rounded (444/1046 = 42.447; csv
    0.4245). Should read 42.4 (still best in column).

11. MINOR / SUSPECTED - Bootstrap independence assumption. Utterance resampling treats 101 GT lines as
    independent, but errors cluster by VAD segment (reverb: whole 28 s segments deleted; Whisper: 384
    insertions on one utterance). Reverb-cell and Whisper CIs are likely too narrow; the K=5 draw-level
    percentile bootstrap is anti-conservative. Suggest a block bootstrap over oracle segments (needs GT line
    to segment mapping) or state the assumption.

12. NIT / CONFIRMED - Wording and rule drift: "20-file subset" is 21 files; "12 runaway" uses an unstated

13. NIT / SUSPECTED - Runner edge cases: _generate labels a 4th consecutive per-minute 429 as "daily quota

14. PROCESS / SUSPECTED - Oracle-segment vs whole-file comparison is disclosed and acceptable, but note
